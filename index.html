    Coaching Agreement & Ethics

    This relationship operates under the International Coaching Federation (ICF) Code of Ethics. Both parties acknowledge the commitments below before coaching begins.

    Our commitments

    1. Clear agreement. Coach and client establish what coaching is and is not, what each party is responsible for, session logistics, and fees — before coaching begins, and revisit it whenever needed.
    2. Confidentiality. Everything the client shares stays confidential, except where release is agreed in writing or disclosure is required by law (e.g. risk of serious harm). This app stores data only in this browser — nothing is sent to a server.
    3. Client autonomy. The client is naturally creative, resourceful, and whole. The client sets the agenda, owns their decisions, and owns the results. The coach champions and challenges; the coach does not direct.
    4. Honesty about the relationship. The coach discloses conflicts of interest, never knowingly misleads, and is clear about qualifications and what coaching can deliver. Coaching is not therapy, counseling, or medical advice — the coach refers out when another professional is needed.
    5. Boundaries. The coach maintains clear professional boundaries and avoids any romantic, sexual, or exploitative relationship with the client.
    6. Right to end. Either party may end the coaching relationship at any point, for any reason, with reasonable notice as set out in the agreement.
    7. Accountability with permission. The coach holds the client accountable to the client's own commitments — boldly, and always in service of the client's agenda, not the coach's.
